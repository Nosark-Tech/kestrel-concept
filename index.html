01 · The report

Read one before you pay for one.

Filter the defects, open any line, and see exactly how an issue is located, rated and explained. The address has been removed; the report structure has not.

  • What was observed

    The valley flashing on the northern roof plane has lifted at the lower third. Water is tracking behind it and staining the ceiling lining in the hallway below. Staining was damp to the touch on the day of inspection.

    Why it’s rated major

    Active water entry into the roof void. Left through a wet season it will reach ceiling battens and the light fitting circuit.

    Moisture readings of 22 to 28 percent were recorded across the southern bearer run, against 12 to 14 percent elsewhere. Two subfloor vents are covered by garden bed soil.

    Sustained moisture above 20 percent in structural timber invites both rot and termite attack.
