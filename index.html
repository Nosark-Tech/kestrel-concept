Sample report · 38 pages Defects section · address removed

  • What was observed

    The valley flashing on the northern roof plane has lifted at the lower third. Water is tracking behind it and staining the ceiling lining in the hallway below. Staining was damp to the touch on the day of inspection.

    Why it's rated major

    Active water entry into the roof void. Left alone through a wet season it will reach the ceiling battens and the light fitting circuit.

  • What was observed

    Moisture meter readings of 22 to 28 percent were recorded across the southern bearer run, against 12 to 14 percent elsewhere under the house. Two subfloor vents on that elevation are covered by garden bed soil.

    Why it's rated major

    Sustained moisture above 20 percent in structural timber invites both rot and termite attack, and it's the condition that undoes a termite treatment.

    PHOTOGRAPH 4.4
    SLOT DELIBERATELY BLANK

  • What was observed

    Mud leads were found on the internal face of two subfloor piers. No live termites were located and the workings sounded dry. The conditions that attracted them, restricted ventilation and a leaking outlet, are still present.

    Why it's rated major

    Previous activity in a property with unresolved conducive conditions. A management plan to AS 3660.2 should be in place before settlement.

    PHOTOGRAPH 5.2
    SLOT DELIBERATELY BLANK

  • What was observed

    The north-east downpipe terminates 300mm above ground with no connection to the stormwater line. Scour is visible in the soil at the footing and the paving adjacent has dropped by roughly 15mm.

    Why it's rated major

    Concentrated water at a footing is the cheapest defect on this list to repair and the most expensive one to ignore.

    PHOTOGRAPH 6.3
    SLOT DELIBERATELY BLANK

  • What was observed

    Flight holes consistent with common furniture borer were noted in three landscaping sleepers along the eastern boundary. The timber is not structural and is not in contact with the dwelling.

    Why it's rated minor

    No risk to the building. Noted so it isn't mistaken for termite damage during a later inspection.

    PHOTOGRAPH 5.8
    SLOT DELIBERATELY BLANK

  • What was observed

    Hairline cracking under 1mm was noted in the mortar bed above the western window head. No displacement across the crack and no corresponding internal cracking was found.

    Why it's rated minor

    Consistent with normal seasonal movement. Worth photographing now so any change over the next two years is measurable.

    PHOTOGRAPH 7.1
    SLOT DELIBERATELY BLANK

  • What was observed

    Batt insulation has been pushed hard against four downlight housings in the living room ceiling. The fittings are not the sealed type rated for insulation contact.

    Why it's rated minor

    Minor to fix, disproportionate in consequence. Twenty minutes in the roof void resolves it.
