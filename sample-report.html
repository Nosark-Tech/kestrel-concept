Major defects

Each one photographed, located, and described in the words you'd use to get a quote.

PHOTO 3.1 · ROOF VALLEY

REF 3.1 · ROOF EXTERIOR

Roof valley flashing failedMAJOR

WHERE
Valley between main roof and rear extension, above the bathroom.
WHAT I FOUND
Flashings lifted at the junction. Active leak staining to the ceiling lining below. Tiles adjacent sound hollow when tapped.
WHY IT MATTERS
Every rain event sends water into the roof void and down into the subfloor. It is the cause of the moisture finding at 4.4.
WHAT NEXT
Licensed roof plumber to reinstate the valley flashing and check adjacent underlay. Quote before settlement.
PHOTO 4.4 · SUBFLOOR

REF 4.4 · SUBFLOOR

Subfloor moisture elevatedMAJOR

WHERE
Southern bearer run, mid-span.
WHAT I FOUND
Moisture meter readings 22–28%. Soil damp. Cross-flow ventilation restricted by stored goods and sealed vents on the south wall.
WHY IT MATTERS
Damp timber is what termites look for. The inactive mudding at 5.2 sits on this same run.
WHAT NEXT
Clear stored goods, reopen vents, and fix the roof leak first. Re-inspect moisture after the next dry spell.
PHOTO 5.2 · BEARER

REF 5.2 · TIMBER PESTS (AS 4349.3)

Inactive termite muddingMAJOR

WHERE
Subfloor bearer above pier 3, southern run.
WHAT I FOUND
Mud leads present. No live termites at the time of inspection. Timber sounds solid outside a 250mm hollow section.
WHY IT MATTERS
Inactive today is not safe. The conditions that brought them in have not changed.
WHAT NEXT
Timber pest management plan from a licensed pest controller, plus the moisture and ventilation works above.
